Test 3 · exploratory, all sessions

After a mega pump, the next candle continues no more often than any candle

A pump is a candle whose move is at least 3σ or 5σ of the previous 120 candles. Amber is how often the next candle moves the same way; slate is the base rate for every pair of candles. When a continuation happens it is brief, and even its full excursion is smaller than the 0.05% + 0.05% taker fees a momentum entry pays.
